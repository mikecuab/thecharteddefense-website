About Mike Coleman

James Michael Coleman, MD

Michael Coleman is a board-certified physician and Hospital Medicine Program Director with US Acute Care Solutions in Alabama, with 13 years of hospital medicine experience across community and health-system settings. As a program director he leads clinical operations and chairs his hospital's Peer Review and Department of Medicine committees — conducting adverse-outcome reviews and defensibility assessments as part of his routine work.

He earned his MD from the University of Alabama at Birmingham and completed his Family Medicine residency at Baptist in Montgomery. Now pursuing formal legal training, Michael brings an authentic MD-to-JD perspective and a working command of how clinical decisions are scrutinized after the fact.
